A computer that lives in your browser.

Fibbe OS has windows, an app store, a browser, mail and 76 apps. It’s free, tiny, and it saves everything on your own computer.

It’s a 90 KB zip file hosted on Google Drive. Windows and Linux versions are coming soon.

Try it: open an app from the dock, then drag its window.

What Fibbe does

A real desktop

Drag windows, snap them to the screen edges, resize them or go full screen. Move the icons wherever you want.

An app store with 76 apps

Games, tools, notes, music and more. Install or remove any of them with one click.

Fibbe Browser

Tabs, a small Fibbe web you can add your own pages to, and real websites that allow it.

Mail and Messages

Get your own @fibbe.mail address and chat with contacts you saved. It all stays on your computer.

Fibbe Shield

Checks links before you open them and scans Fibbe’s notes, mail and messages for scam wording.

Child accounts

A parent PIN, blocked website categories, approved-sites-only mode, locked apps and a daily screen time limit.

Your own Wi‑Fi

Make networks with a name and password, then connect and switch between them.

An on-screen keyboard

On a computer, a keyboard appears and lights up each key as you type.

Backups

Export your whole account to a file and bring it back whenever you need it.

A 17-step setup

Pick your language, Wi‑Fi, account type, email, web protection, theme and starter apps the first time you open it.

Questions

Why does the download go to Google Drive?

The file is hosted there. After you click Download, you land on Google Drive’s download for the zip.

Is it a real operating system?

It’s a full desktop experience that runs inside your browser. It doesn’t replace macOS, and it isn’t a native Mac app, so macOS shows no warnings.

Where is my data saved?

In the browser you open Fibbe with, on your own computer. Open it in the same browser each time, and use Settings, then Export backup, to keep a copy.

Does it need the internet?

Fibbe runs from the file you downloaded. Real websites in Fibbe Browser need an internet connection, and some sites don’t allow being shown inside other pages.

Can children’s accounts be bypassed?

Fibbe saves its settings in the browser, so someone who clears the browser’s site data can get around them. Treat Parental Controls as a helper, not a lock.

Does Fibbe Shield protect my whole computer?

No. It checks links opened in Fibbe Browser and the notes, mail and messages saved inside Fibbe.

What about Windows or other computers?

Fibbe is made for macOS 15 and newer. Windows and Linux versions are coming soon. Until then, any computer with a current browser can open the file, but those setups aren’t officially supported.

For parents

A Fibbe child account is managed with a parent PIN. You choose what it can open, and Fibbe keeps a log of what it blocked.

Blocks dangerous sites

Links that aren’t encrypted, use raw IP addresses or look-alike names are stopped.

Blocks the categories you pick

Adult content, gambling, violence, social media, games and shopping.

Strict mode

Only sites you approve can be opened.

Safe searching

Adult searches are blocked.

Screen time

Set a daily limit. When it runs out, only the parent PIN adds more time.

Locked apps

Terminal, Task Manager and Settings need the PIN, and so can installing new apps.

Set it up

  1. In the setup, choose “Child account” in step 3.
  2. In step 7, enter your name and a PIN you’ll remember.
  3. In steps 8 to 13, pick the protection level, categories, sites, screen time and locked apps.
  4. Change anything later in the Parental Controls app. It asks for your PIN.

Good to know

Fibbe saves its settings in the browser, so someone who clears the browser’s site data can get around them. The keyword lists are short and will miss some sites. Treat Fibbe as a helper, and keep talking with your child about the web.
