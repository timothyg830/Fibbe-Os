FIBBE OS for Mac (macOS 15 or newer)
=====================================

HOW TO OPEN
1. Double-click the zip to unzip it (macOS does this for you).
2. Open the "Fibbe OS" folder.
3. Double-click "Fibbe OS.html". It opens in your browser (Safari works great).
4. Click the window and use the full screen button to fill your screen.

TIPS
- Your account, mail and notes are saved in the browser you opened it with.
  Open it in the same browser every time. Use Settings > Export backup to keep a copy.
- Some real websites in Fibbe Browser may stay blank, because those sites don't
  allow being shown inside other pages. Use "Open in new tab" for those.
- This is the web version of Fibbe in a zip. It is not a native Mac app,
  so macOS won't show any "unidentified developer" warning.

Enjoy Fibbe!
